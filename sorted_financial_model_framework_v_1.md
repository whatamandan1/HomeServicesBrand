# Sorted - Financial Model Framework (V1)

# 1. Financial Strategy Overview

Sorted is being designed as a:
- recurring revenue business
- operationally leveraged marketplace
- AI-assisted workforce orchestration platform
- multi-brand subscription ecosystem

The financial model prioritizes:
- recurring monthly revenue
- locality density
- operational efficiency
- low fixed staffing overhead
- scalable contractor utilization
- high customer lifetime value

The long-term objective is to build:
- predictable recurring cash flow
- scalable operational margins
- low marginal expansion cost
- strong working capital dynamics
