# Sorted - High Level Business Plan

## Business Overview

Sorted is a technology-driven home services platform focused on recurring subscription-based services.

The business operates as:
- a lead generation platform
- a managed marketplace
- a workflow orchestration platform
- a customer relationship management platform

The first launch brand will be:
- GardensSorted

Future expansion will include additional home service brands operating under the Sorted platform.

Examples may include:
- CleaningSorted
- RepairsSorted
- WindowsSorted

All brands will operate from a shared centralized technology platform.

---

# Core Operating Model

Sorted acquires customers directly through:
- online marketing
- local targeting
- recurring subscription offers
- brand-led customer acquisition

Customers subscribe to recurring home service plans.

Sorted manages:
- customer relationships
- customer support
- scheduling
- workflow orchestration
- payments
- provider coordination
- operational oversight

Service delivery itself is completed by:
- self-employed local contractors

Providers operate as independent contractors while receiving:
- recurring work opportunities
- centralized operational support
- customer allocation
- scheduling coordination

---

# Customer Model

Customers will:
- subscribe online
- manage accounts through customer portals
- communicate through centralized support
- access multiple services through one account over time

The platform will prioritize:
- low-friction signup
- recurring subscriptions
- convenience
- operational reliability
- centralized communication

Recurring service continuity with the same provider will be preferred where operationally possible.

---

# Provider Model

Providers will:
- onboard digitally
- operate as self-employed contractors
- claim or receive work allocations
- work within locality-based territories
- primarily communicate through WhatsApp initially

The provider model is designed to:
- minimize operational friction
- maximize recurring work consistency
- maintain flexible workforce scaling

Provider performance will be monitored internally through operational scoring.

---

# Technology Platform

The business is being built as:
- a modular technology platform
- a workflow-driven operations system
- an AI-assisted management platform
- a multi-brand infrastructure

The platform will support:
- customer accounts
- recurring subscriptions
- provider management
- scheduling
- workflow automation
- operational CRM
- AI customer support
- communications orchestration

The backend platform will remain:
- service agnostic
- brand agnostic
- scalable for future vertical expansion

---

# AI and Automation Strategy

AI systems will assist with:
- customer support
- workflow orchestration
- scheduling assistance
- operational recommendations
- communication handling
- escalation triage

The operational model is designed so that:
- AI handles routine operational tasks
- human admins manage escalations and oversight

The long-term objective is to maintain:
- low operational overhead
- scalable automation
- centralized operational control

---

# Revenue Model

Primary revenue source:
- recurring customer subscriptions

Additional future revenue opportunities may include:
- premium subscription tiers
- annual plans
- one-off services
- provider tools/services
- cross-sold services across brands

The business is designed around maximizing:
- recurring revenue
- customer retention
- operational efficiency
- locality density

---

# Initial Launch Plan

Initial launch focus:
- Yorkshire, UK
- residential customers
- recurring gardening services

The initial objective is to:
- establish localized provider networks
- build recurring customer density
- refine operational workflows
- optimize AI-assisted operations

The platform architecture is designed to support future:
- geographic expansion
- service expansion
- operational scaling
- additional home service brands

---

# Long-Term Vision

Sorted aims to become:
- a centralized home services operating platform
- a multi-brand recurring services ecosystem
- an AI-assisted operational marketplace infrastructure

The long-term platform strategy supports:
- multiple services
- shared customer identity
- cross-service expansion
- recurring customer relationships
- operational automation at scale
